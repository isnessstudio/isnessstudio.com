Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how Isness Studio LLC (“we”, “us”) handles information on this website and in our games and apps. Each game may add its own short privacy notice in the App Store and inside the game.

1. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer you, and we delete them when they are no longer needed.

2. Our games and apps

Depending on the game, we may process:

  • Account data: a player name and an account ID, and an email address if you choose to sign in with one.
  • Game data: scores, match results, progress and settings, so the game works and you can play across devices.
  • Purchase data: confirmation of in-app purchases. Payments are handled by Apple, Google or our payment provider; we never see your full card details.
  • Technical data: device type, operating system, app version and crash reports, to fix problems.
  • Advertising data: if a game shows ads, our ad partners (for example Google AdMob) may use a device identifier. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in the game settings.

3. Why we use it

To run the games, keep accounts and matches fair, process purchases, fix bugs, show ads you agreed to, and meet legal duties.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us run the games (hosting, app stores, payment and ad partners), and only as needed for that purpose, or when the law requires it.

5. Where data is stored

Our servers are in the European Union and in other regions close to players. Data may be transferred between countries with appropriate safeguards.

6. How long we keep it

We keep account and game data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it longer.

7. Children

Our games are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or KVKK), you can ask to see, correct, delete or export your data, or object to its use. Email info@isnessstudio.com with the subject “Data request”.

9. Changes

We will post any change on this page with a new effective date.
